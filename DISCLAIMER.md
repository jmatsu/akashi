## Self-hosting

If your use or your constraints need more than that — an audited build, a
network you control, a deployment that outlives ours — host Akashi yourself.
`dist/` is static files, so any host will do.

Self-hosting is unsupported. Questions, issues and requests about your own
deployment will not be answered.
